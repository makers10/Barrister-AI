Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies Barrister Pro uses and why. Barrister Pro uses only one cookie, and it is strictly necessary for the Service to function.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser by a website. Cookies can serve many purposes — from keeping you logged in, to remembering preferences, to tracking your browsing for advertising.

Cookies we use

Cookie name Purpose Type Duration Data stored
session Links your browser to your current document analysis session so the server knows which uploaded document belongs to you. Strictly necessary (functional) Session (deleted when you close your browser) A randomly generated identifier. No personal data.

Do we use tracking or advertising cookies?

No. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. We do not track you across websites.

Do third-party services set cookies?

The following third-party resources are loaded by the page:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — loads fonts. Google may log the request, but does not set tracking cookies via this request.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — loads Font Awesome icons. Cloudflare may log the request for CDN performance purposes.

Neither of these services sets cookies in your browser from the context of our site.

Do you need to give consent for our cookie?

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and general best-practice guidelines, strictly necessary cookies (those required for the basic operation of a service) do not require separate consent. Our single session cookie is strictly necessary — the Service cannot function without it. We therefore do not display a cookie consent banner.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block the session cookie, Barrister Pro will not work correctly because the server will not be able to link your uploaded document to your browser session.
