The Process

From PDF to insight in three steps

No setup, no training, no expertise required. Just upload and get answers.

  1. 01

    Upload Your PDF

    Drag and drop any legal PDF — contracts, agreements, NDAs, policies. Up to 200MB. The system automatically identifies the document type from 12 categories.

  2. 02

    Intelligence Engine Indexes

    The document is chunked, semantically indexed, and stored in a vector database. Every clause, section, and page is mapped so answers are pinned to their exact source.

  3. 03

    Get Your Analysis

    Run any of the five analysis modes or ask questions in plain English. Every finding cites the exact page and section it came from — no hallucinations, just facts.
