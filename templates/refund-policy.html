Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026.

This Refund Policy applies to any paid plans or purchases made through Barrister Pro.

1. Current Service Status

Barrister Pro currently offers a free tier. No payment is required to use the basic features of this Service. This Refund Policy will apply if and when paid plans are introduced.

2. Paid Plans (when applicable)

When paid plans are available:

  • Subscription plans: You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future billing but does not generate a refund for the current billing period already paid.
  • Pay-per-use credits: Unused credits purchased within the last 7 days are eligible for a full refund if you have not used any of those credits.
  • Technical failure refunds: If a paid analysis fails due to a verified technical fault on our side and we cannot resolve it within 48 hours, we will refund the cost of that specific transaction.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided for:

  • Completed analysis where the Service functioned as described
  • Dissatisfaction with AI-generated output quality (we cannot guarantee accuracy — see our Terms and Conditions)
  • Subscriptions cancelled after the billing period has started where service has been provided
  • Credits used or partially used

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email support@barrister.pro within 7 days of the transaction, with:

  • Your order or transaction reference number
  • The reason for the refund request

We will respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

5. Consumer Rights Under Indian Law

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits your statutory rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 (India) or any other applicable consumer protection legislation.

6. Contact
