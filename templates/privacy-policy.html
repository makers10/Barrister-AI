Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. Effective date: July 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Barrister Pro ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our AI-powered legal document analysis service at this website ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Barrister Pro is an AI-powered legal document analysis tool. For questions about this policy, contact us at: support@barrister.pro.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the Service:

  • Uploaded PDF documents — files you upload for analysis. These are processed to extract text and generate AI analysis. They are temporarily stored on our servers during processing and deleted after your session ends.
  • Session identifier — a randomly generated, non-personal token stored in a functional session cookie. This links your browser to your current analysis session only.
  • Server logs — standard web server logs including IP address, timestamp, and HTTP request information. These are used for security monitoring and deleted within 30 days.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, payment information, or any other personal details unless you voluntarily contact us.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process your uploaded PDF and return AI-generated analysis to you.
  • To maintain your session while you are using the Service.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or security threats.

We do not use your documents to train AI models. We do not sell or rent your data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party providers to function:

  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) — your document text is sent to OpenRouter's API to generate AI analysis. OpenRouter's privacy policy applies to this processing. We send only the document text, not your identity.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Your browser IP address is shared with Google solely to deliver these fonts.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — Font Awesome icons are loaded from Cloudflare's CDN. Your browser IP address is shared with Cloudflare solely to deliver these icons.

5. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary session cookie. This cookie:

  • Contains only a randomly generated session identifier (no personal data)
  • Is required for the Service to function — it links your uploaded document to your browser
  • Is deleted when you close your browser
  • Is not used for tracking, advertising, or analytics

Because this is a strictly functional cookie, it does not require your consent under applicable law.

6. Data Storage and Security

Uploaded documents are stored temporarily during your session and are deleted when your session ends or the server restarts. We do not retain your documents beyond the active session. Server access logs are retained for up to 30 days for security purposes.

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights Under India's DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf

To exercise any of these rights, contact: support@barrister.pro. We will respond within 72 hours.

8. Children

This Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights: support@barrister.pro
